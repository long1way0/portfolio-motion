About

At 13 I got into graphics and video editing and picked up the basic principles of animation, but didn't yet know where to apply them. Over time I tried many directions and realized that movement is what hooks me most, so I moved into motion graphics.

I'm a motion designer now: I make animation, promo and visuals for brands, digital products and events. I have a basic grasp of UX/UI, so I understand how animation works inside an interface and helps the user. I use Claude Code, Codex and Kling AI at work: I build projects with them and automate routine, so more time goes into the graphics itself.
